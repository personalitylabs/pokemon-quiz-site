Project 1 By Emmanuel Lee

Dear Shau,

I guess it's my turn to confess. These few months with you have been one of the most fun and amazing segments of my life. When you initially confessed to me, I had no idea how this would play out. I was just happy that someone liked me for being myself. I've always loved you as a person because I could feel that you really value and appreciate the people in your life and I greatly respect that. And I really enjoyed spending day and night with you even before you confessed because you're one of the funnest people to be around. I can just forget my troubles and have fun whenever I'm with you. But if you ask me what made me look at you romantically, it was probably seeing the different sides of you that made me realise how cute you can be (and no this is not just about you screaming in horror Minecraft). The more time we spent together, the more certain I was that you were who I wanted to be with and that you were perfect for me. Even now, you continue to prove that to me with your constant reassurances, support, and most importantly, your best efforts at communicating your feelings have shown me just how genuine you can be. All this makes me trust you and know that I don't have to be afraid to love you, because you're worth it. I know this took a long time, and I'm sorry to have kept you waiting for so long. This project took way longer because I kept spending all my free time with you. I also wanted to show you that there are reasons for my actions, and that sometimes, being patient can be worth it in the end.

If we get together, I can't promise you a perfect relationship. The future is full of uncertainties, and there will almost certainly be times where we both get hurt, or upset with each other, due to misunderstandings and disagreements. Also, people can change. As unlikely as it may seem now, we may one day decide that we simply aren't right for each other and end up on separate paths.

Despite those risks, Shau, if you're still willing to stay by my side, what I can promise is that I will do my best to make our ideal relationship, one where we learn from mistakes and grow alongside each other, into a reality.

So, will you be my girlfriend?

Love from,
Emmanuel Lee
